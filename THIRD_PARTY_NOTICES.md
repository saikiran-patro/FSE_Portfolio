# Third-party assets and interaction patterns

The skill cloud adapts the Fibonacci sphere and depth projection from [Magic UI Icon Cloud](https://magicui.design/docs/components/icon-cloud). The mobile dock follows the [Magic UI Dock](https://magicui.design/docs/components/dock) interaction pattern, with touch and reduced-motion adaptations. Magic UI is MIT licensed: https://github.com/magicuidesign/magicui/blob/main/LICENSE.

Technology SVGs in src/Assets/skills are monochrome logos from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), fetched from https://cdn.simpleicons.org/ in the portfolio's orange palette. Logos and names remain the property of their respective owners.

The RealPage SVG is the official orange-mark logo obtained from https://s.realpage.com/dist/site/static/logos/rp-logo-2022.svg. It identifies the employer in Sai Kiran's professional experience. RealPage retains its trademark rights.

Lucide icons are provided by the existing lucide-react dependency under the ISC license.

Resume claims and evidence links come from the user-supplied resume; prior Hardware Expo, GitHub, and YouTube achievements are retained from the existing portfolio.

The contact map adapts [Magic UI Dotted Map](https://magicui.design/docs/components/dotted-map), using the [svg-dotted-map](https://github.com/thejessewinton/svg-dotted-map) dependency.
The RealPage wordmark is recolored to the portfolio orange at the owner's request; the SVG has a transparent background.
